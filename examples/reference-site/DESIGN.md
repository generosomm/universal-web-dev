# Design

Direction: Taste "The page is the object" (seed 1120079840). The idea: **the printed tide table pinned by the net-store door becomes the page**; each section is another piece of that stationery, and the one live thing is the low-tide window for your day.

Tokens: ground `#133621`, surface `#1a4429` (card tones up to `#2b663e`, all at least 4.5:1 with the ink), ink and accent `#f4d96c`, muted `#b3a55e`, accent ink `#133621`. Paper panels use the ink colour with green text. Type: Bricolage Grotesque only; five steps (small, body, lead, head, display).

Components: filled panels with shaped edges (tabs, scallops, dotted perforations), no outlines, no shadows at rest. One button shape: square, solid gold, green text. Square image corners, one photo treatment.

Accessibility: visible focus ring in the ink colour, 16px+ body text, semantic headings on every section, reduced-motion fallback that keeps content.
