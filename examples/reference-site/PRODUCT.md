# Product

Problem: a small guesthouse needs one page that makes a stranger want to stay and tells them how to ask for a room. There is no booking system.

Users: travellers deciding where to stay; the owner, who answers booking emails by hand.

Critical flow: read what the place is → compare the four rooms and prices → check when the lighthouse walk is possible → send a booking email with the room named.

MVP acceptance:
- The first screen says what Saltrum is and offers one action.
- All four rooms show their bed, view and nightly price (sample).
- Choosing a date shows that day's low-tide walking windows, labelled as a sample model, not navigation advice.
- Every booking action opens an email with the room or request filled in; nothing pretends to take payment.
- Works on a phone and without JavaScript (content and email links still usable).

Non-goals: online payment, availability calendar, accounts, analytics.
