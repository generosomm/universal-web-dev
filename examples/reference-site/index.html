A letter from the house

This building stored nets for sixty years. You can still see the hooks in the beams of the Trawl room, and the floor slopes towards the old hoist door so the water could run out.

We kept what we could. The walls are tarred pine, the windows are where the net menders wanted light, and the stove in the loft is the one they warmed their hands at.

Most guests come for the quiet and stay for the walk. Twice a day the sand comes up and you can reach the lighthouse dry-footed; we will tell you when at breakfast.

Bring a warm jumper. The sauna does the rest.
